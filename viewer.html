Trajectory viewer

Recorded episodes, step by step

Each episode replays the screen before every action with the agent's reasoning, the GUI action or app-native tool call it chose, and what the tool returned. The Bench and new-app groups were run by Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview on MobileGym++. The OpenMobile-Data group samples the released training trajectories.

OpenMobile-2

An open stack for mobile agents: a simulated playground of commercial apps, the trajectories and verifiable tasks collected on it, and the agents trained from them.
